📊 Dashboard Design Atlas

📌 1. Dashboard Types (by function)

🏢 Strategic Dashboard

Long‑term health · C‑Suite & Board

🎯 High‑level KPIs / KRIs
✨ Motto: "Are we winning?" – aggregated snapshot.

🔍 Analytical Dashboard

Data exploration · Trends & predictions

📈 Granular · High interactivity
🧠 Designed for exploration, not quick glance → “Why is this happening?”

⚡ Operational Dashboard

Real‑time monitoring · Immediate alerts

⏱️ Seconds latency · Live feeds
🔥 “What needs attention now?” — DevOps & call centers.

🎯 Tactical Dashboard

Department tracking · Campaign optimization

📅 Weekly/daily batches · comparisons
📈 Bridges strategic & operational: line‑of‑business managers.
